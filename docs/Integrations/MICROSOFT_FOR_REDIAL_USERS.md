# Connect your Microsoft account to Redial

For Redial users: home users, business teams and Live Agents. Connect Outlook, OneDrive and Teams so Redial works with the Microsoft apps you already use. This works with personal accounts (Outlook.com, Hotmail, Live) and with work or school Microsoft 365 accounts.

> **Status:** coming soon. These features are being built. This page shows how they'll work.

## What you can connect

| Turn on | What Redial does | What Microsoft asks you to allow |
|---|---|---|
| **Outlook Calendar** | Bookings, appointments you agree to on calls, and Live Agent shifts appear on your Outlook calendar. Changes you make in Outlook come back to Redial. | Have full access to your calendars |
| **Outlook Mail (send)** | Emails you write in Redial are sent from your own Outlook address. | Send mail as you. Redial can't read your mail. |
| **Outlook Contacts** | Your contacts' names show on incoming calls, and you can import them into Redial. | Read your contacts |
| **OneDrive, Word and Excel** | Save recordings, transcripts and summaries, and export call logs, leads, or Live Agent hours and earnings as Word and Excel files. | Have full access to Redial's own folder in your OneDrive (Apps → Redial). Redial can't see the rest of your OneDrive. |
| **Microsoft To Do** | Action items from calls become tasks (later). | Create and edit your tasks |
| **Teams** | Teams meeting transcripts come into Echo (later, work accounts only). | Read meeting transcripts. Your company's IT admin must approve this one. |

You can also use **Sign in with Microsoft** to sign in to Redial.

## Connect

1. In Redial, go to **Settings → Connected accounts → Microsoft**.
2. Click **Connect Microsoft**, sign in, and review what Redial is asking for.
3. Click **Accept**.
4. Choose which calendar to sync, then turn on the other features you want.

## Work account? Your IT admin may need to approve Redial

If you see "Need admin approval", your company only allows apps its IT team has approved. Send your IT admin:
- this page;
- the approval link from Redial's support team (support@redial.si). It lets them approve Redial for everyone at your company in one step.

Admins can approve Redial for just the features you need. Teams transcripts always need admin approval.

## Disconnect

- **In Redial:** go to **Settings → Connected accounts → Microsoft → Disconnect**. Redial stops syncing and deletes the keys it kept for your account.
- **In Microsoft:**
  - personal accounts: **account.microsoft.com → Privacy → Apps and services → Redial → Stop sharing**;
  - work accounts: **myapps.microsoft.com → Redial → Remove**, or ask your IT admin.

Events and files that were already synced stay where they are.

## How Redial handles your Microsoft data

- Redial uses your Microsoft data only for the features you turn on.
- Redial doesn't sell it, use it for ads, or use it to train AI models.
- Your connection keys are stored encrypted, and only Redial's servers can use them.
- Questions: support@redial.si.
