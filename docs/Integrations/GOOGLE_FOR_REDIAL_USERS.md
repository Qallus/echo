# Connect your Google account to Redial

For Redial users: home users, business teams and Live Agents. Connect your Google account so Redial works with the Google apps you already use. You choose what to turn on, and you can disconnect at any time.

> **Status:** coming soon. These features are being built. This page shows how they'll work.

## What you can connect

| Turn on | What Redial does | What Google asks you to allow |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Calendar** | Bookings, appointments you agree to on calls, and Live Agent shifts appear on your calendar. Changes you make in Google Calendar come back to Redial, so you're never double-booked. | See and edit events on your calendars, and see your list of calendars |
| **Gmail (send)** | Emails you write in Redial (follow-ups, replies to callers) are sent from your own Gmail address, so replies come to you. | Send email on your behalf. Redial can't read your mailbox. |
| **Google Contacts** | Your contacts' names show on incoming calls, and you can import contacts into your Redial contacts. | See your contacts |
| **Google Drive** | Save call recordings, transcripts and summaries to a Redial folder in your Drive. | See and edit only the files Redial creates or the ones you pick. Redial can't see the rest of your Drive. |
| **Google Docs** | Turn a call or meeting summary into a Google Doc. | Covered by the Drive permission above |
| **Google Sheets** | Export your call log or leads. Live Agents can export hours and earnings. | Covered by the Drive permission above |
| **Google Slides** | A meeting recap as slides (later). | Covered by the Drive permission above |
| **Google Meet** | Bring your Meet meeting transcripts into Echo (later). | See your Meet meeting records |

You can also use **Sign in with Google** to sign in to Redial. That only shares your name, email and photo.

## Connect

1. In Redial, go to **Settings → Connected accounts → Google**.
2. Click **Connect Google**, choose your Google account, and review what Redial is asking for.
3. Click **Continue** (Google may say **Allow**).
4. Back in Redial, choose which calendar to sync, then turn on the other features you want. Each one asks for its own permission the first time you turn it on.

**Using a work Google Workspace account?** Your company's IT admin may need to allow Redial first. If you see "Access blocked" or "admin approval required", send them a link to this page.

## Disconnect

- **In Redial:** go to **Settings → Connected accounts → Google → Disconnect**. Redial stops syncing and deletes the keys it kept for your account.
- **In Google:** go to **myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps and services → Redial → Delete all connections**.

Events, files and emails that were already synced stay where they are. Delete them yourself if you want them gone.

## How Redial handles your Google data

- Redial uses your Google data only for the features you turn on.
- Redial doesn't sell it, doesn't use it for ads, and doesn't use it to train AI models.
- AI is used only to do what you asked, such as writing a summary or adding an appointment from a call.
- Your connection keys are stored encrypted, and only Redial's servers can use them.
- Redial's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
